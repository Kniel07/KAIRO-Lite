Version: 1.1 (amended)
Status: Approved
Owner: Kniel Ranches

---

# 1. Vision

KAIRO-Lite is an AI-native Personal Operating System that transforms scattered ideas, notes, projects, conversations, and knowledge into an organized, searchable, and continuously improving knowledge base.

Rather than acting as a chatbot, KAIRO-Lite serves as an intelligent thinking partner that augments human decision-making.

Its goal is to become a second brain capable of:

- Capturing ideas
- Organizing knowledge
- Preserving context
- Connecting information
- Assisting implementation
- Supporting long-term learning

---

# 2. Product Goals

Primary Goals

- Eliminate fragmented notes.
- Centralize project knowledge.
- Provide AI-assisted thinking.
- Reduce repeated prompting.
- Preserve historical decisions.
- Improve knowledge retrieval.
- Support structured documentation.

Success Metrics

- Fast knowledge retrieval
- Accurate context recall
- Low duplicate information
- Consistent documentation
- Reliable AI recommendations
